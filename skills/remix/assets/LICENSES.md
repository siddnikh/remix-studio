# Bundled asset licences

All retrieved 2026-11-05. Licences as stated on each source page at that date. Neither source page's claim has been independently verified.

## Font

`fonts/Anton-Regular.ttf`: Anton, Copyright 2020 The Anton Project Authors. SIL Open Font License 1.1, full text in `fonts/Anton-OFL.txt`. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/anton

`fonts/Archivo-ExpandedBlack.ttf`: Archivo, Copyright 2020 The Archivo Project Authors. SIL Open Font License 1.1, full text in `fonts/Archivo-OFL.txt`. A static instance (width 125, weight 900) cut with fontTools from the variable font, because ffmpeg's drawtext cannot select a variable font's axes. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/archivo

## Music

`music/unbeatable-arcade-machine-165s-45s.mp3`: a 45 s stream-copied excerpt (from 165 s) of "Unbeatable Arcade Machine" by HoliznaCC0, album "All The Years I've Haunted, Part 1". CC0 1.0. Tagged Rock, Lo-fi, Instrumental. Source: https://freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0/all-the-years-ive-haunted-part-1/unbeatable-arcade-machine-1

## Sound effects

`sfx/*_owlish.wav`: from "Sound Effects Pack" by OwlishMedia, Impacts folder. CC0. The author says these are sounds recorded over the years. The pack's separate Synth folder is not included. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/sound-effects-pack

`sfx/*_artisticdude.wav`: from "Swishes Sound Pack" by artisticdude. CC0. The author describes recording them by swinging a hanger and sticks past a camcorder microphone. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
